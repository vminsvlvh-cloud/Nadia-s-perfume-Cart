V22 HOME ANNOUNCEMENT BAR
At the top of index.html there is an editable What's New / الجديد الحصري bar.
Tap the pencil icon to edit Arabic text, English text, and its link.
Edits are stored in localStorage on the current device.
For public updates visible to every visitor, edit the default values in index.html and redeploy, or later connect an admin backend/CMS.
